About

How I work

I learn by building complete systems: the database, the API, the data pipeline and the model. Three rules I follow on every project:

  1. 1
    Tests first, or it isn't finished.My larger projects ship with test suites. In one, a test caught a bug that would have wiped all the data before it reached anyone.
  2. 2
    Report what I measured, even when it looks bad.Each project lists its real numbers, plus a short note on what went wrong or what didn't work.
  3. 3
    Own my part, understand the parts around it.On team projects I take one layer, then learn enough of the others to keep the connections between them clean.
